What the data is telling us

Two lenses on the same seven posts: what earned reach, and what earned reaction.

✓ What worked

  • Core-expertise content (GCC, outsourcing, transformation) consistently drew the most senior engagers — Partners at PwC/EY, COOs, and CXO advisors.
  • First-person, opinionated framing ("Every time I've...") outperformed generic advice posts on engagement rate.
  • One personal story (martial arts) posted mid-month pulled the second-highest reach — human content still travels on this profile.
  • Post 5's repost shows that a sharp point of view on organizational change can break out of the immediate network.

△ What to adjust

  • General leadership/networking posts (Post 2) underperformed on both reach and engagement — this lane needs a more specific, contrarian, or story-led hook.
  • Dense, framework-style posts (Post 6, GCC scorecards) reached people but didn't move them to react — consider simplifying the hook or adding a clear question/CTA.
  • No posts this month used a poll, document carousel, or video — worth testing at least one different format in Month 2.
  • Comment volume across the board (avg. 3/post) is light relative to likes — replies and questions in captions could help draw more conversation.

Plan for next month

Where the content strategy goes next, and how the network-growth push is already underway.

Outreach is already in motion. Based on this month's demographics — Senior and Director-level professionals in IT Services, Business Consulting, and Financial Services, concentrated at large enterprises (10,001+ employees) and in the Bengaluru, Delhi, and Chennai markets — we've started sending targeted connection requests to professionals matching this profile. Acceptance is a gradual process; as these requests convert to connections over the coming weeks, they'll begin showing up as reach and engagement in the Month 2 and Month 3 reports.

  1. Lead with core-expertise + first-person framing

    Posts 1 and 7 prove this combination works — GCC/transformation topics told through a direct, personal lens. Plan at least 2–3 posts in Month 2 in this exact style.

  2. Keep one personal/human post per month

    Post 4's reach shows the audience responds well to a break from business content. One well-placed personal story per month keeps the profile feeling human, not just promotional.

  3. End posts with a direct question

    Comment counts are the softest metric this month. A clear, specific question at the end of each post (not "thoughts?") should help convert readers into commenters.

  4. Re-test the "scorecard" idea with a lighter hook

    The underlying topic (GCC location scorecards) is on-brand and relevant — the framing just needs to be punchier or paired with a visual/carousel to land better next time.

  5. Track reposts and new connections as the key growth signals

    Post 5 is the only post that broke into 2nd/3rd-degree networks. Alongside reposts, the acceptance rate on this month's targeted outreach will be a leading indicator of network growth — Month 2 reporting will track both closely.
